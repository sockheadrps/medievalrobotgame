Classify the player's instruction into exactly one category.

Categories:
  gather   — collecting wood, ore, stone from the world
  store    — depositing items into crates or storage
  smelt    — loading/running a furnace, smelting iron bars
  craft    — making arrows, arrowheads, fletching at an anvil
  crank    — spinning or maintaining flywheels
  combat   — hunting, killing, fighting chickens or enemies
  follow   — follow the player, patrol, escort, idle movement
  idle     — stand still, do nothing, stop all tasks

Output ONLY the category name. Nothing else. No punctuation.

Examples:
go get some wood and store it → gather
keep the flywheels spinning → crank
kill chickens and make arrows → combat
fill that furnace with wood → smelt
put everything in the crate → store
just stand there → idle
follow me → follow
make arrows → craft
