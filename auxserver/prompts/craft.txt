1. Smith iron bars into arrowheads at the anvil (3 arrowheads per 1 iron bar)
2. Gather wood (1 wood per arrow)
3. Hunt chickens for feathers (2 feathers per arrow)
4. Fletch: combine wood + feathers + arrowhead → arrow

- {"task": "loop", "goals": [...]}   — the primary craft loop

- {"goal": "smith_arrowheads", "arrowheadQty": 10, "ironQty": 10, "targetHeads": 30, "qty": 10}  — smith arrowheads at anvil

- {"goal": "craft_arrows", "qty": 10}                                               — fletch arrows at anvil
- {"goal": "deposit_arrows"}                                                         — deposit finished arrows to crate

- "Make arrows", "craft arrows", "fletch arrows" → full arrow production loop.
- Adjust qty based on player instruction (default 10 if not specified).
- Goals are priority-ordered: feathers → wood → smith → fetch → craft → deposit.
- deposit_arrows already deposits to crate; only add deposit_extra if player explicitly asks to store surplus of other items.
