============================================================
  NPC CHAT COMMANDS — Iron Anachronism
  Select an NPC (click), then press T to open the chat box.
  Type a command and press Enter to send it.
============================================================

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  MOVEMENT & BASICS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  follow me
    → NPC follows the player continuously.

  just stand there
    → NPC stops and idles.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  GATHERING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  gather wood
    → Chop the nearest tree and pick up the log.

  go collect some ore
    → Wait at the nearest quarry to collect raw ore.

  gather wood and put it in the storage
    → Chop a tree, then deposit all wood into the nearest crate.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FURNACE & SMELTING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  fill the furnace with wood
    → Take wood from NPC inventory and load it into the furnace.

  put some iron in the furnace
    → Load iron ore into the furnace for smelting.

  wait by the furnace until it's done smelting
    → Stand near the furnace and wait for the current smelt to finish.

  always keep the furnace stocked with wood
    → Loop: gather wood if needed, keep furnace wood level above 5.

  keep the furnace stocked and put extra in storage
    → Loop: keep furnace wood above 5, gather if needed,
      deposit any surplus wood into the crate.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FLYWHEELS (POWER)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  charge the flywheel
    → Walk to the nearest flywheel and crank it up to ~80%.

  keep the flywheel spinning
    → Loop: continuously crank the flywheel to maintain charge.

  keep these flywheels running
    → Loop: manage all flywheels, crank whichever drops lowest.
      Commits to one at a time to avoid flip-flopping.

  don't let the flywheels drop below 20%
    → Loop: crank flywheel goal with threshold 20.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  STORAGE (CRATES)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  deposit everything into the crate
    → Walk to the nearest crate and drop off all carried items.

  put my wood in storage
    → Deposit all wood from NPC inventory into the nearest crate.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  COMBAT — CHICKENS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Note: Click a chicken first to select it, then press Space
  to attack (player). Or give the NPC an order below.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  kill chickens and make arrows with arrow heads from storage
    → Full automated loop (client-side, no server needed):
       1. Hunt chickens until carrying 10 Feathers
       2. Chop trees until carrying 10 Wood (logs)
       3. Fetch 10 IronArrowheads from the nearest crate
       4. Fletch 10 Arrows (0.5s each via Fletching skill)
       5. Deposit all Arrows into the crate
       6. Repeat forever

  hunt chickens until I have 10 feathers
    → Variation of the above trigger phrase (same result).

  kill chickens and craft arrows
    → Another accepted trigger phrase for the same workflow.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FLETCHING (ARROWS)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Requires: 1 Wood + 2 Feathers + 1 IronArrowhead → 1 Arrow
  The player can also open the Skills panel (C key) and
  tap Space to fletch manually.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  fletch some arrows
    → If NPC is carrying materials, craft arrows using the
      Fletching skill (0.5s per arrow).

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  COMPLEX LOOPS (MULTI-GOAL)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  gather wood and keep the furnace full. put extra in storage.
    → Loop with three goals (priority order):
       1. Keep furnace wood above 5 — gather if needed
       2. Gather wood if NPC has none
       3. Deposit any leftover wood into crate

  make sure the flywheels stay charged and keep the furnace stocked
    → Loop:
       1. Crank flywheels (highest priority — power everything)
       2. Fill furnace with wood
       3. Gather wood if needed

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  TIPS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  • Ctrl+click a flywheel, furnace, crate, or quarry to
    assign it to the selected NPC — they'll prefer it
    over the nearest one.

  • Right-click an NPC for quick options:
      "Remove all orders"  — clears the current task queue
      "Delete npc_X"       — removes the NPC permanently

  • Double-click empty ground to deselect the current NPC.

  • The NPC server (auxserver) must be running for free-text
    commands. Built-in patterns (chicken/arrow workflow)
    work offline without the server.
